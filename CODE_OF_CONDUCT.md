# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

REPFLOW welcomes contributors, reviewers, and users from diverse backgrounds. We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and professional environment focused on better fitness technology and sound engineering.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to communicate clearly, discuss ideas on their technical merits, welcome thoughtful feedback, respect different perspectives and experience levels, and protect the privacy of others.

| Do                                               | Do not                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Offer specific, constructive feedback.           | Harass, threaten, or demean others.              |
| Challenge ideas with evidence and empathy.       | Attack people instead of discussing work.        |
| Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions. | Share private information or credentials.        |
| Help maintain an inclusive environment.          | Use discriminatory, sexual, or hostile language. |

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed respectfully and handled in proportion to the circumstances. The owner may request changes, remove content, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

By participating in REPFLOW, you agree to uphold these standards.
